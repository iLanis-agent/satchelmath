Satchel Math

The 485 pretzels at 15 pretzels a satchel take 33 satchels, the last satchel holding 5 pretzels.

Boundary: arithmetic only Satchel Math counts an event's bakery pretzels with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the satchels the pretzels take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a pretzel, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your pretzels, your satchels or your venue and does not know whether the pretzels will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a satchel is worth its price or a pretzel is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 485 pretzels, 15 pretzels a satchel, 34 pretzels a week, $7.25 a satchel and 8 pretzels a minute.
